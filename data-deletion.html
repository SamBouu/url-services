Data Deletion Policy

Last Updated: April 17, 2025

1. Introduction

This Data Deletion Policy explains how you can request the deletion of your personal data collected by [Your App Name]. We respect your privacy rights and have procedures in place to handle deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws and our Privacy Policy.

2. How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to sami.boudechicha@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request.
  2. Include your username, email address, and which social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, X, Telegram) you wish to delete verification records for.
  3. Optionally provide a reason for your request.

3. What We Delete

Upon verification of your identity, we will:

  • Remove your technical and usage data from our servers.
  • Flag your verification records as deleted in our off-chain index.

4. Immutability of Blockchain Records

Verification proofs stored on the World Chain blockchain are immutable and cannot be removed. However, we will mark those entries as deleted in our off-chain directory so they will not appear in future queries by other users.

5. Response Time

We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days of receipt. You will receive confirmation once your data has been processed.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or the status of your request, please contact us at support@yourdomain.com.
