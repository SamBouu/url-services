Welcome to [Your App Name] ("App", "we", "our", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App that allows you to connect and verify your social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, X, Telegram) with World ID on the World Chain blockchain.

2. Information We Collect

  • Social Media Account Data: When you connect a social media account (Facebook, Instagram, X, Telegram), we collect your user ID, username, profile picture URL, and verification status only. No posts, messages, or private content are accessed.
  • World ID Verification Data: We collect your public World ID identifier and verification timestamp to record on the World Chain.
  • Technical Data: We automatically collect device and usage data (e.g., IP address, browser type, operating system) to operate and secure our service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Verification Processing: To verify that you own the connected social media accounts and to record this status on the World Chain.
  • Display Purposes: To allow other users to view which accounts have been verified on the World Chain.
  • Service Improvement & Security: To monitor and improve our App, troubleshoot issues, and protect against fraud or abuse.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may disclose data to comply with legal obligations or enforce our Terms of Service.

5. Data Storage & Retention

Verification records are stored immutably on the World Chain blockchain. Technical and usage data are retained for up to 12 months for diagnostic and security purposes, then anonymized or deleted.

6. Your Rights

  • Access & Correction: You can view or update your profile data by disconnecting and reconnecting your social media accounts.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your verification records by contacting support. Note that blockchain entries may remain immutable; we will flag them as deleted in our off-chain index.
  • Privacy Settings: Manage connected accounts at any time in the App settings.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard measures (HTTPS, encryption, secure key storage) to protect your data. However, no system is fully secure; use caution when sharing sensitive information.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our App is not intended for individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
